# Changelog

## 0.7.1 (2026-10-30)

Built on 0.7.0 during Lex's in-game pass of 2026-10-30, from his tests and a players' request: the name plates, the law, the pickpockets and the subtitles. None of the night run's mind work is in it (that waits on its own branch).

### Pickpockets (Lex, 2026-10-30, in game)

- The thief walks straight up to you. His walk was given anew several frames in a row every 3 s, and each new one stopped him, so he walked, stopped and walked again, and from 20 m off gave up before he reached you. He is sent on only when he stands with no walk running.
- He gets close enough to take it: the bump counts from 2 m (was 1.3, and the walk stops him short), standing within 4 m he is sent the last step, and a far start gets 1.5 s a metre more before he gives up; the give-up line says how near he came.
- A pickpocket works on foot: a man with his own horse within 25 m is never picked (the game had one ride up to you and off with the cash), one who gets on a horse anyway gives up, and the bump needs you both on foot.
- Nothing else takes him on the way: the pickpocket on his way to you and on the run counts as busy for the rest of RealNPCs (the test cast's stroll had one back at his spot, 7 m off, until he gave up), and he is sent on whenever he comes no nearer for 3 s.
- His dot turns red even when he already had one (the test cast's minute mark is taken off and a red one made; anyone else's dot is turned red).

### The law: self-defence holds (Lex, 2026-10-30, in game)

- Shooting the pickpocket who robbed you made you wanted, and the lawmen near fought you. RealNPCs only looked at the wanted level, which stays 0 while the law is after you, so it never cleared it; and the game's pursuit, once started, comes back however often it is cleared. Now RealNPCs sees the pursuit and puts the bounty back, and while a fight with someone who came at you is on (90 s after your last hit on them) and you've hurt nobody else, it holds witnesses back from calling the law and has lawmen already fighting you let go. In game (2026-10-30) the game's own witnesses still set off to report the killing a few seconds after it, hold or not: stopping them is the next thing to solve, and the log now says which crime the game charges. A body found later by a passer-by still starts a search for an unknown suspect.
- Bumping into someone on the way no longer spoils it: a stumble within 2 m of you counted as hurting someone who hadn't come at you (the mind's shove, 0.7.0), which ended self-defence for five minutes; the game charges no crime for it. It stays a slight in people's minds, and if they answer it, you started it. A real blow still counts.
- The log's new `LAW` lines say each time you become wanted or your bounty changes, and what RealNPCs saw at the time (a fight in self-defence, the game's charge, a despairing shot near). `LAW stage` lines follow the game's own stages (a witness on the way to tell, witnesses active, the law investigating, wanted, the red circle's radius) and name who near you carries a map mark. Not yet all seen in game.
- A search that dies down while the law can't see you is roadmap 27: the game keeps no score a mod can lower for it, and clearing the wanted level doesn't end a search.
- For testing: three seconds after the test cast arrives, `TESTCAST after arrival` logs what each of them is doing (calm, fleeing, running), whether you have a gun out, the dead within 30 m and the law's stage.

### What people say near you is a subtitle, not a line at the bottom (Lex, 2026-10-30: "There shouldn't be inner thoughts showing at the bottom of the screen")

- The words of a street scene, a hello or goodbye, the doctor's visit, the law chase and the pickpocket's "Pardon me" were drawn on the bar at the bottom, where they read as thoughts (a mourner's "Last night I dreamed he walked in the door"). They now show as they're said in the subtitle box drawn the game's way ("Hermine: \"...\""), within `[Ticker] ScenesOnTickerMetres` of you (12 m, was 30: Lex saw a mourner's line from 26 m off, out of sight; an ini still on 30 is moved to 12 at settings version 120); `[Output] Subtitles = off` hides them, and a line said to you is never covered by one. The bar keeps the news; thoughts stay over heads. Voicing these lines in the speakers' own voices is roadmap 1. Not yet seen in game.

### The Mod list page (Lex, 2026-10-30: "to be able to turn on/off other mods from the settings page")

- A last page on the settings panel lists the other mods: the Script Hook .NET scripts in the scripts folder (enRAGE among them) and the ASI plugins in the game folder, each on or off, with its .ini named as its settings when there is one. Enter, then Enter again, turns one off (its file renamed with ".off" after it, so the game passes it over) or back on; nothing is deleted. A script takes it with the next Insert reload, an ASI plugin only when the game next starts, and the page says which. What modding stands on (Script Hook, its .NET hook, the ASI loader, Lenny's mod loader, the .NET API and Newtonsoft.Json) and RealNPCs itself aren't listed. Not yet seen in game.

### Reset RealNPCs Memory at a station clerk (Lex, 2026-10-30: "the NPCs very quickly come to have very high fear and anger levels with the player")

- At a station agent or clerk, the list with Telegraph, Train Tickets and Pay Bounty also offers Reset RealNPCs Memory: hold Q (a pad's RB) for two seconds and everyone forgets what they know and feel about you, while the world stays (Lex's pick). In RealNPCs: your name to them, the talks, sightings, hearsay, the deeds of yours they saw or heard of, their memories of you, their fondness and courting (sweethearts too), your deeds and the town's talk of them, kin riding in for revenge, word still waiting to spread, and the dead you killed no longer yours. With enRAGE installed: its townsfolk's fear, fondness, grudge and favours, what they saw of you and knowing you at all, and the strangers' grudges building toward a nemesis; enRAGE's nemeses and their hate are left as they are. The people, the regulars, families, diaries already written and the ties between people stay. Copies of RealNPCs' save and of enRAGE's people, world and nemeses go to `RealNPCs\backup` first; if a copy fails, nothing is forgotten. Without enRAGE, RealNPCs' part alone. `[Features] ResetAtClerk` (on; settings version 121); also on the Testing page (Everyone forgets you). The test cast is let go by it (bring them again). Not yet seen in game.

Met is greeting, talking, flirting, any contact, or their speaking up to you; only seeing you do something no longer counts. enRAGE's nemeses and the game's own named people (sheriffs, the story cast) keep their names.

- RealNPCs' subtitles sat on the game's own subtitle line and the two overlapped. Ours now start just under the game's line and grow down toward the news bar.

New: `[Display] NameCardsSecondLine` (on; settings version 117), Personality line under the names on the same page: off shows the name alone, without the temper, trade, "Stranger" or gang under it. Not yet seen in game.

## 0.7.0 (2026-10-29)

Built in the night run of 2026-10-28 (Lex's brief), the day after it and the in-game pass that evening, on the 0.6.3 release. RealNPCs now stands alone (no enRAGE needed, compatible with it); most of what follows was seen working in game on 2026-10-29. The mind stays in shadow: everything new that would change what people think, say or do sits behind `[Mind] MindLive` (off), and with it off every path runs as it did in 0.6.3, except the two players' reports below: live changes Lex asked for during the run, one commit to take back.

### Names over heads (Lex, 2026-10-29: "enRAGE did a really good job of placing NPC names over the NPC head that looked really good and our mod wasn't doing this")

- People RealNPCs knows carry their name over their head in the game's title type, the way enRAGE drew its cards: the name, a smaller line under it (a temper, a trade, "Stranger", or a nemesis's hate: Wary, Sore, Vengeful, Murderous, coloured gold to red), no box, steadied as they walk, hidden behind walls and in other rooms, fading out near the edge of the range and smaller with distance. Who gets one: enRAGE's records and townsfolk when it runs, and RealNPCs' own people always: the town regulars, anyone who has thought over their head, spoken with you or taken part in a street scene. `[Display] NameCards` = off | known | everyone (everyone adds what a stranger looks like, "A townsman", in grey; names over every passer-by wait for RealNPCs' own naming by look), `NameCardsMetres` (30, strangers) and `NameCardsKnownMetres` (75, people you know). While enRAGE runs with its own cards on, ours stay off unless `NameCardsBesideEnrage`. Adapted from enRAGE's card drawing with its author's permission. Settings version 97; F7 `TEST name cards:`.
- The game's own named people have their names too (Lex, the same afternoon: the sheriff of Valentine had none): the story characters (Sheriff Malloy, Sheriff Leigh Gray, Dutch, the Braithwaites and the Grays, some three hundred), the named bounty targets, and the uniques the game names by their job and town ("Valentine deputy", "Rhodes clerk"), from a table of our own adapted from enRAGE's. The line under the name is what the model says of them ("Valentine sheriff"), their temper, or "Wanted" for a bounty; enRAGE's own notes on them stay its.

### Standing alone, step 1 (Lex, 2026-10-29: RealNPCs is to need enRAGE less and less, and one day not at all)

- RealNPCs now loads with enRAGE absent. Before, with `NemesisMod.dll` gone from `scripts`, Script Hook .NET couldn't construct the add-on at all (`ScriptHookRDRDotNet.log`: `Could not load file or assembly 'NemesisMod'`), because its code named enRAGE's types throughout. Now enRAGE's people are held behind thin handles of our own and every read or write of them goes through one file, compiled only when it's reached; the game's date and the model names, which came from enRAGE's code, are our own (adapted from it, with credit). With enRAGE installed nothing changes: the same values are read and written the same way. Without it the add-on starts, logs that enRAGE isn't running and waits for it, as it did when enRAGE was merely slow to load; running without enRAGE was the next step, below.
- RealNPCs runs without enRAGE (step 2, the same day). With no `NemesisMod.dll` in `scripts` it starts at once standing alone, and when enRAGE is installed but never comes up it starts after the two minutes it always gave it; the log's Ready line and the first notice say so and list what waits for enRAGE (its nemeses and their records, its townsfolk's names, the feeling bars, the grudge meter, interrogation, revives, avengers, its newspaper items, its settings page). Everything of RealNPCs' own runs: the townsfolk and regulars, ties, memories, street scenes, the mind in shadow, letters and diaries, the ledger, stalkers, the test cast. The towns and regions come from the game's own map zones through a table of our own, so the ticker, the thoughts and the scenes know where they are without enRAGE. Word of mouth and diary entries bound for one of enRAGE's people wait, and are not lost, until enRAGE is back; the dead and the romances on enRAGE's roster are left alone meanwhile, so installing enRAGE later finds its people as RealNPCs last saw them. The readmes now say the add-on works best with enRAGE and runs without it. The town regulars wear their own clothes standing alone (the first run in game, 2026-10-29 14:32, left them undressed: the dressing waited on asking enRAGE whom it had looked at, and with no enRAGE the answer is nobody); and the F7 notice counts RealNPCs' own townsfolk and regulars where it said "0 people in the world" (the count was enRAGE's roster); and a town regular whose body streams out of the world stays away three minutes before another body may be theirs (standing alone every body may be cast, and one regular came back 36 times in an hour, hopping from body to body).

### Manners of 1899

- A friend is someone you've had two scenes with, or warmth enough; one hand up or one round bought no longer makes a man who grieves you for three weeks, drinks over you, condoles and takes up your grudges.
- A respectable woman doesn't drink herself drunk in a saloon, stand the house a round, or spit; the saloon's own women may. A woman who steps in on a bully shames him, and he answers her as a lady.
- Strangers aren't on first-name terms: in a scene between people with no tie and no town in common, it's "mister" and "ma'am".
- People think of a named person as he or she, not they; "You're welcome", "Take care" and "I'll be seeing you" are gone; a friend's condolence doesn't set a place at the table.
- The old men have twelve talks and the sweethearts eight quarrels, so no two pairs repeat word for word.

### Strangers at a campfire

- At night out of the towns, two or three men sit round a fire off the road and one calls you over: sit a spell on foot and he tells a tale over coffee, the others chiming in; ride on and he mutters; a gun on them and they beg off. Now and then a traveller walks in from the dark and is made room for. `[Encounters] Campfires` (on), `CampfireChance` (15); the Testing row `campfire`.

### The voice below the floor (West World)

Next morning the town has a word of it. Nothing is ever found. `[Encounters] VoiceBelow` (on, with West World); the Testing row `below`.

### The doctor's patient

- By day in a town, someone whose child has the fever walks to the doctor's and back, thinking it over on the way (hope going, the doctor's word inside, what's left of it coming home). `[Scenes] DoctorsPatient` (on); the Testing row `patient`.

### The quarrel ladder, played (with [Mind] MindLive)

- With `[Mind] MindLive` on, an argument or a fight between two people the mind knows no longer runs on a fixed script: the quarrel ladder (words, a shove, blows, a gun drawn) decides how far it climbs and how it comes down, from the two natures and the surroundings: the hour and the place, drink, the guns on their belts, the law in sight, a friend of either close enough to step in (he does, as the scene's third), the crowd watching, their history and who was wronged, and what it's over (a debt is money, a feud honour; a saloon's drink; the street's a look over nothing). It ends by an apology, a joke, backing down, walking off, a friend's word, a drink stood in a saloon, a man down, or the law looking on. Guns come out only where they did before (both armed, no law near, out of the saloon); the shot is never fired: the standoff breaks off and the log says the ladder called for it. `[Mind] QuarrelLadder` (on, under MindLive); off, or with MindLive off, exactly the fixed scenes. The log's `LADDER` line gives the whole run; F7 `TEST ladder:`. The Testing page's Street scenes row "A quarrel from the ladder (turns: words, blows)" fires one on demand (an argument from words, then a brawl from the blows), needing only `[Mind] MindShadow`.
